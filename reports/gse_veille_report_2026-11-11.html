Executive summary
The week's dominant signal is Guangtai's five-year SATS tender win and innovation collaboration in Singapore, which hands a Chinese competitor the region's most influential ground handler account and a reference for every APAC tender that follows. In parallel, the completed Korean Air-Asiana merger creates a 236-aircraft carrier with a rationalising fleet, while Incheon's 100% eco-friendly GSE mandate by 2030 converts Korea into a hard-deadline electric replacement market forecast to nearly double to USD 745 million by 2034. Southeast Asia demand drivers remain strong, with Thai Airways holding 80 aircraft on order, Garuda unlocking Danantara funding and Bangkok Airways expanding its ATR fleet. TLD must respond on two fronts simultaneously: defend the Singapore and ASEAN handler channel against Guangtai, and win the Korean post-merger standardisation and electrification programme before specifications are locked.
