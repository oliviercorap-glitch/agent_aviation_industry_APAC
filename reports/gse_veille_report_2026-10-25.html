Executive summary
The dominant signal this week is competitive: Guangtai has formalised a collaboration with SATS, the region's largest handler, while simultaneously marketing a full-range electric GSE portfolio and exhibiting at GHI, and CIMC Tianda has booked a PTE Asia 2027 stand to push cargo handling into Southeast Asia. On the demand side, Korean Air's 103-aircraft Boeing order and its 1 January 2027 Asiana integration create the largest fleet-driven GSE opportunity in North Asia, while Vietnam's carriers are expanding at record pace and Indonesia's GSE market is forecast to grow 49% to USD 1.72 billion by 2031. TLD's response must be two-track: defend the SATS and Korean Air accounts with total-cost-of-ownership and leasing propositions within days, and capture the Vietnam and Indonesia growth windows with rental fleet deployment. Margin risk is rising from rare-earth and semiconductor constraints on e-GSE costs, which could erode the premium that underpins TLD's electrification strategy.
