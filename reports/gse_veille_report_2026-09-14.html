Executive summary
This week's signals are dominated by competitive threat rather than demand: both Guangtai and CIMC Tianda are named HIGH-threat competitors actively expanding across Southeast Asia, with Guangtai pushing a full-line GSE portfolio and Tianda winning boarding bridge contracts that anchor broader airport accounts. On the demand side, Cathay Pacific's AI contrail trials and extended Middle East flight suspension point to a Hong Kong hub prioritising decarbonisation while trimming widebody utilisation near term, and Taiwan's tax revenue surge improves the fiscal backdrop for airport capex. The net read is that TLD's APAC growth is constrained less by demand than by aggressive Chinese pricing and bundling in price-sensitive markets. We must defend the Southeast Asian bridge and full-line GSE franchise immediately while positioning electric and connected equipment as the differentiator in Hong Kong and Taiwan.
