Executive summary
This week's APAC signals are dominated by three forces: aggressive Chinese competitor positioning, major handler consolidation, and a wave of airline fleet expansion across Southeast Asia. CIMC Tianda has committed to PTE Asia 2027 in Singapore, confirming a structured long-term push into TLD's core Southeast Asian territory, while Swissport's joint venture entry into Indonesia signals that global handlers are consolidating procurement in the region's largest GSE market. Simultaneously, Korean Air's absorption of Asiana, Vietnam Airlines' 12-widebody lease tender, Vietjet's 22-aircraft delivery wave, Vietravel's 50-Airbus order and Bangkok Airways' ATR expansion are generating a dense pipeline of fleet-driven GSE demand over the next 12-24 months. The strategic imperative is speed: TLD must lock key accounts into framework and lease agreements before Chinese competitors and consolidated handlers set the terms. We recommend immediate engagement with Swissport Indonesia, Korean Air post-merger procurement, the Long Thanh greenfield opportunity and Vietjet's urgent capacity gap, while preparing a competitive response to Tianda at PTE Asia 2027.
