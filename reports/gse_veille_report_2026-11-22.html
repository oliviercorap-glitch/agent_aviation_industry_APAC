Executive summary
The dominant development this week is Guangtai's five-year SATS tender win and formalised innovation partnership, which entrenches a Chinese competitor at Changi and creates a reference account that will be cited across APAC handler tenders. Simultaneously, Textron's $1.2 billion divestment of TUG, Douglas, Premier and Safeaero creates a rare window of competitor uncertainty in tractors and towbarless equipment. Demand-side signals are strongly positive: Incheon targets 100% eco-friendly GSE by 2030, Korean Air has closed the Asiana acquisition, AOT has added a third Suvarnabhumi handler, and Garuda is reactivating its fleet with sovereign wealth backing. TLD should defend the SATS account with a differentiated leasing and lifecycle offer, attack Textron accounts during its ownership transition, and build a Korea electrification plan anchored on the 2028 65% milestone.
