Executive summary
The week's dominant signal is competitive: Weihai Guangtai has secured a five-year GSE tender with SATS in Singapore and is simultaneously positioning hard on electric GSE and building European brand credibility, directly threatening TLD's core APAC accounts. On the demand side, Korean Air's completed $1.3bn Asiana acquisition creates a consolidated mega-carrier buyer in Korea, while AOT's appointment of AOTGA as a third Suvarnabhumi handler and Thai Airways' fleet expansion to 150 aircraft open significant new procurement opportunities in Thailand. TLD should respond by defending Changi, engaging the new Korean and Thai buying centres before frameworks close, and accelerating its connected and electric GSE proposition.
