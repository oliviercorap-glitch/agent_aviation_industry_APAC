Executive summary
The dominant signal this week is Guangtai's five-year SATS tender win in Singapore, which hands our principal Chinese competitor a flagship APAC handler reference and a pricing anchor for the whole region. In parallel, Korea is becoming the highest-value opportunity in APAC ex-China, with the Korean Air-Asiana merger completing on 17 December 2026 and a record 103-Boeing order driving a consolidated multi-hundred-unit GSE replacement cycle into 2027. Southeast Asia fleet expansion is broad-based, with Vietnam Airlines planning up to 30 widebodies, Thai Airways ramping A321neos to 32 and Indonesia confirmed as the region's largest GSE value pool. Against a 12.17% regional CAGR, the risk is not market size but margin: Chinese manufacturers are exporting aggressively into Southeast Asia while battery cost declines lower their barrier to competitive electric offerings. We must respond to SATS within 48 hours, lock in Korean relationships before the merger closes, and convert TLD's leasing and electrification capability into differentiated APAC offers rather than competing on unit price.
