Executive summary
The dominant signal this week is Guangtai's five-year SATS tender win in Singapore, which converts a collaboration into a multi-year anchor account at Changi and gives the Chinese manufacturer a credible Southeast Asian reference to attack TLD's Indonesian, Thai and Vietnamese positions. Guangtai is simultaneously investing in European trade-show presence, signalling a professionalised global brand push that erodes the Western-premium argument TLD relies on. On the demand side, APAC fundamentals remain strong: Korea's market is forecast to nearly double to USD 745m by 2034, Indonesia grows to USD 1.72bn by 2031, and Korean Air's 103-Boeing order plus the 17 December Asiana merger will produce a large but consolidated Korean tender in 2027-2028. Japan is shifting decisively toward electrification and automation, with ANA targeting 4,000 of 14,000 GSE units for decarbonisation and JAL expanding Level 4 autonomous towing at Haneda. The priority is to defend share through service, financing and technology rather than price, and to lock in early positioning with ANA, Korean Air and Garuda before Chinese competitors set the specification.
