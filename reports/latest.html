Executive summary
The dominant signal this week is Guangtai's five-year SATS tender win in Singapore, a direct competitive loss at a flagship APAC hub that will accelerate Chinese credibility across Southeast Asia. This is reinforced by Guangtai's sustained European trade-show investment and the persistent presence of CIMC Tianda, confirming a structural price and reference-account threat. On the demand side, Korea is the standout: the Korean Air-Asiana merger completing in December plus a record 103-Boeing order create a concentrated GSE procurement window, while Indonesia's 14-airport modernisation and USD 1.7bn market trajectory offer the largest growth pool. Japan and South Korea both show 8-9% CAGRs with quality-sensitive procurement that favours TLD over Chinese entrants. The priority is to contain the SATS loss, secure position in the Korean consolidation and Indonesian modernisation, and accelerate electrification differentiation before Chinese OEMs close the gap.
