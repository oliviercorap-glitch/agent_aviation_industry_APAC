Executive summary
The most urgent signal this week is the reported SATS trial of Weihai Guangtai advanced GSE in Singapore, which threatens TLD's position with one of APAC's largest ground handlers and could cascade across SATS' regional network. Guangtai's pushback tractor demand recovery and the confirmed presence of both Guangtai and CIMC Tianda at GSE Expo Europe and PTE Asia 2027 confirm a sustained, well-funded Chinese commercial offensive across APAC. On the demand side, Thailand's THB 67.3 billion Suvarnabhumi ground handling and cargo concession, Indonesia's 7.8% historical GSE growth and Korea's 8.7% forecast CAGR create substantial procurement opportunities, while the Korean Air-Asiana merger on 17 December 2026 will concentrate Korean purchasing into a single account. TLD must respond simultaneously on defence, countering Guangtai at SATS, and on offence, positioning for the Thai, Indonesian and Korean opportunities with electric and leasing-based offers.
