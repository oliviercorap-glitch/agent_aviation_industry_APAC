Executive summary
The week's decisive signal is competitive: Weihai Guangtai has secured a five-year GSE tender with SATS in Singapore, explicitly built around electrification, while simultaneously positioning globally for electric demand and operating its own financial leasing arm — meaning it now competes on product, finance and service rather than price alone. US tariff proposals are injecting FX and supply-chain volatility across Vietnam and Singapore, which will slow some pipeline conversion and may push Guangtai to price more aggressively in APAC to offset lost Western volume.
